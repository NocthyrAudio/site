Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

At Nocthyr Audio, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have regarding your information.

1. Who we are

Nocthyr Audio is a professional audio production project. We offer mixing, mastering, original soundtracks and related tools and services.

2. Information we collect

We only collect data that is necessary to provide our services and communicate with you. This includes:

  • Contact form data — When you voluntarily send us a message through the contact form, we collect your name, email address and the content of your message.
  • Technical data — Basic anonymized information such as browser type, device type, approximate location (country), and pages visited, collected through privacy-friendly analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics or similar tools that do not use personal tracking cookies).

We do not collect sensitive personal data, payment information (handled by external stores), or track you across other websites.

3. How we use your information

The information you provide is used exclusively for:

  • Responding to your messages and requests
  • Providing the services you asked for
  • Improving the website experience and performance
  • Complying with legal obligations when required

We will never sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Contact form

When you submit a message through our contact form, you consent to us receiving and storing the information you provided (name, email and message) for the purpose of replying to you. This data is kept only as long as necessary to handle your request and is not used for any other purpose.

5. Cookies and analytics

We may use essential and privacy-focused analytics tools that do not rely on invasive tracking cookies. If non-essential cookies are ever used, you will be asked for consent through a cookie notice. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

6. Data storage and security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you send us against unauthorized access, loss or misuse. Access to contact messages is limited to the site operator only.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time (for example, by asking us to delete your contact message)
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority if you believe your rights have been violated

To exercise any of these rights, simply contact us using the details below.

8. Third-party links

Our website may contain links to external stores or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the updated date.
